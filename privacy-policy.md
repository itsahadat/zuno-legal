# Zuno: Daily Planner & To Do — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 November 2026  
**Developer:** BangLabs  
**Contact:** dev.sahadat@gmail.com

Zuno is a to-do list and routine planner for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## 1. Your tasks stay on your phone
Tasks, notes, reminders, routines, routine history and focus sessions are stored **only on your device**. Zuno has no account and no cloud sync. We (the developer) do not receive, sell or share this data.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include Zuno's data in your own device backup. That backup is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

## 2. Ads
The free version of Zuno shows banner ads served by **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:
- your device's advertising ID,
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- information about ad views and taps, and basic device information.

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, Zuno asks for your consent (via Google's consent message) before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings › Ad privacy choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 3. Voice input
If you tap the microphone in quick add, your phone's speech recognition service turns your words into text. Zuno receives only the resulting text. The audio is handled by that service under its own privacy policy.

## 4. Permissions
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Show your reminders |
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | Ring reminders at the exact minute |
| Full-screen notifications | Show the alarm screen for alarm-style reminders |
| Run at startup | Restore your reminders after the phone restarts |
| Internet / network state / advertising ID | Ads only (added by Google Mobile Ads SDK) |

## 5. Export
When you use Settings › Export, the file is saved to the location you choose. We never receive it.

## 6. Deleting your data
Items you delete are removed permanently after 30 days. Uninstalling Zuno deletes all of its data from your phone.

## 7. Children
Zuno is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## 8. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL and in the app with a new effective date.
